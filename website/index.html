Four rules. Every layer.

Each rule documents the real rendering bug it prevents, and was validated on open-source projects.

no-physical-properties--fix

Physical → logical

margin-left, text-align: left, float, ml-4, rounded-l, style={{ marginLeft }}. Knows your Tailwind version and rtlcss.

directional-icons

Arrows that point back

ChevronRight, ArrowLeft, fa-arrow-right from lucide, heroicons, MUI, Tabler, Font Awesome… without an RTL flip.

bidi-unsafe-string

Scrambled strings

@handles, C++, signed numbers and phone numbers in JSON, ARB, YAML, PO, JS and JSX. Understands ICU, i18next, printf and Rails placeholders.

missing-dir-attribute--fix

Missing direction

<html lang="ar"> without dir, dynamic lang with hardcoded dir, inputs and user comments without dir="auto".
